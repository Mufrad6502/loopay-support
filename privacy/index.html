YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Loopay is operated by Mufrad Mustahsin. For privacy questions or requests, contact rankfitpro@gmail.com.

Information we handle

Account information. Sign-in providers and Supabase process your account identifier and email address, which may be an Apple private relay address. The iOS app requests your email and Apple account identifier, not your full name. Loopay does not receive your Apple password.

Your subscription records. We store service names, prices, currencies, billing cycles, payment or trial dates, categories, status, reminder preferences, colors, and notes that you enter. Records are associated with your account so you can retrieve and manage them.

Purchase verification. For lifetime purchases, we process store transaction references, product and environment information, account binding identifiers, verification status, and entitlement status. Apple handles payment. We do not receive your full card number or bank account credentials.

On your device. The app stores session information and preferences locally. Reminders are scheduled on your device after you grant notification permission. Your operating system controls their delivery and presentation.

Support and operations. We receive information you include in support messages. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical request information, including IP addresses and security or error logs, to operate and protect the service.

Currency preferences and exchange rates

Your preferred currency is saved to your account. The app requests public USD-based exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API and cache them on your device. No account identifiers, subscription names, or amounts are sent in that request. The provider receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Rates are used to estimate converted displays; original billing records remain intact.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, save and display plans, calculate estimates, provide requested reminders, verify or restore purchases, answer support requests, and prevent unauthorized access or receipt reuse.

Loopay does not include advertising SDKs or use your app activity for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell your subscription records.

Service providers and storage

Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and server functions. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, and platform services. Support messages are handled through our email service. Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

The current Supabase project is hosted in Mumbai, India. Providers may process operational or support information in other countries. Data is transmitted through encrypted connections, and access to account records is restricted by authenticated ownership rules. No security measure eliminates every risk.

Retention and your choices

Subscription records remain associated with your account until you delete them or request account deletion. You can edit or delete individual plans in the app and disable notifications in device settings. Operational records, backups, and records needed for security or legal obligations may remain for the periods applicable to those systems and obligations.

Delete your account in Loopay Settings and confirm with Apple. This removes your active account, subscription records, and associated purchase-verification rows from our database and revokes the Apple authorization for Loopay. If you create a new account with the same Apple identity, a store-verified purchase can be restored separately; deleted subscription records are not restored. For access, correction, or deletion assistance, contact support from your account email. We may verify ownership. Never send passwords or sign-in codes. Deleting a Loopay record does not cancel billing with a service provider. Apple separately controls its purchase records and refund process.

Children

Loopay is a general-purpose subscription organizer and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information without appropriate authorization so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Policy updates

We will update this page when our practices change and provide additional notice when appropriate. The effective date above identifies this version.
