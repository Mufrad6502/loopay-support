THE ESSENTIALS

Good to know.

Add your first plan

Sign in with Apple to save plans to your account, or explore the sample-data demo. Tap Add subscription and enter the service, amount, billing cycle, and next payment date. Demo changes aren't saved to an account.

Get a heads-up before renewal

Allow notifications in iOS Settings, then enable reminders in Loopay. Choose the renewal day or 1, 3, or 7 days before. Keep dates up to date and open Loopay periodically to refresh upcoming reminders.

Track a trial

Set a plan's status to trial and enter its end date. Enter the price that applies after the trial; this amount is included in projected spending.

Understand the numbers

Spending is estimated from the plans you enter, including active trials. Weekly and yearly costs are converted into monthly equivalents. Currencies stay separate. Cancelled-plan figures show potential savings, not verified money saved.

Cancel a service

Cancel with the service provider first, then update Loopay. Deleting or marking a plan cancelled in Loopay does not stop the provider from billing you. Loopay does not connect to your bank or automatically discover charges.

Free access and purchases

New accounts receive 60 days of free access. The planned release offers a one-time lifetime unlock for continued editing, with the price shown before purchase. Purchase and restoration testing is still being completed in the current beta.

When purchases are available, restore using the original Loopay account and the Apple account used to purchase. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Your account and data

You can delete individual plans in their editor. To request account or data deletion during the beta, email support from your account email. We may verify ownership before acting. In-app account deletion is being completed before public release.

Tell us what happened

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description. Remove private information from screenshots. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment card details.
